What we believe

Commercial success doesn’t mean creative compromise.

We believe artists deserve to be paid fairly for their work without being forced into generic commercial projects that compromise their creative identity.

Pattern connects artists with opportunities that value their voice while helping them build sustainable careers.

Who we work with

Artists building something uniquely their own.

We are not looking for artists who chase trends. We are looking for artists building something uniquely their own.

  • A distinct visual language — work that is unmistakably yours.
  • A strong body of work — a portfolio that holds together.
  • Professional communication — clear, reliable, easy to work with.
  • Ambition to grow their practice — you want to go further.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do I have to work exclusively with Pattern? +

No. Artists remain independent and are free to pursue their own opportunities.

What commission does Pattern take? +

Commission varies depending on project scope and services provided.

Do you guarantee work? +

No. We focus on building opportunities and making introductions, but we cannot guarantee project volume.

What kind of artists do you represent? +

Artists with distinct visual identities across painting, murals, illustration, installation, mixed media and related disciplines.
