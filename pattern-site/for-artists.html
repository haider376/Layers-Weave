What we look for

A point of view, and the craft to back it.

  • Distinct visual identity — work that is unmistakably yours.
  • Strong portfolio — a body of work that holds together.
  • Professional communication — clear, reliable, easy to work with.
  • Ambition — you want to grow and take on more.
  • Reliability — you deliver what you commit to.
  • Originality — you make work the world hasn’t seen.
What we offer

Opportunities, support and a community.

  • Paid opportunities — commissions and projects with real budgets.
  • Project management — we handle logistics so you can make.
  • Content production — professional documentation of your work.
  • Events and exhibitions — moments to show and be seen.
  • Brand collaborations — introductions to businesses that value you.
  • Community — a network of artists building careers together.
