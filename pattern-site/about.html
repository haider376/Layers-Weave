About Pattern

Connecting artists, businesses and creative opportunities.

Helping artists build sustainable careers while creating memorable spaces through art.

Our story

Pattern was created to bridge the gap.

Pattern was founded by artist Alexander Krige after experiencing first-hand how difficult it can be for artists to secure meaningful opportunities while maintaining creative independence.

Too often talented artists spend more time chasing work, managing projects and promoting themselves than focusing on their craft. At the same time, businesses struggle to find original artists who can help create memorable spaces and meaningful customer experiences.

We connect businesses with carefully selected artists, helping create artwork, installations and creative experiences that benefit both the artist and the client.

Our manifesto

We believe culture belongs everywhere.

We believe culture belongs everywhere. Not only in galleries, museums and private collections, but in cafés, restaurants, bars, hotels, workplaces and public spaces. Art should be experienced as part of everyday life.

Artists deserve to be paid fairly for their work without being forced into generic commercial projects that compromise their creative identity.

We help artists build sustainable creative careers by creating opportunities, managing projects and connecting them with people who genuinely value their work.

Founder

Alexander Krige

Founder & Artist

Alexander is a London-based artist with over 10 years of experience in graffiti and mural work. Throughout his career as an independent artist, he has created murals internationally, held exhibitions, and delivered workshops in schools and community spaces.

He founded Pattern after recognising a disconnect between artists and businesses. Businesses were looking for attention, originality and cultural relevance, but often struggled to find authentic artists and creative projects that could deliver meaningful results. At the same time, many artists were forced to take on work that did little to develop their practice, acting more as decorators than creative partners.

Pattern was created to solve that problem by connecting artists with businesses that genuinely value original creative work.
