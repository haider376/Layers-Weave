About

Artists build worlds.

Artists see the world differently and transform that perspective into something others can experience. Pattern exists to connect artists with opportunities while helping businesses access original creative talent.

Mission

Make original creativity accessible — and sustainable.

We believe spaces are more memorable when they’re shaped by real artists, and that artists deserve opportunities that pay fairly and respect their independence.

Pattern sits in the middle: matching businesses with the right talent, managing the work end to end, and building careers in the process.

Founder story

It started with a frustration.

Pattern was founded by artist Alexander Krige after experiencing first-hand how difficult it can be for artists to secure meaningful opportunities while maintaining creative independence.

Having sat on both sides — making the work and trying to find the rooms for it — he built the kind of partner he wished had existed: one that brings artists real opportunities and gives businesses access to original talent.
